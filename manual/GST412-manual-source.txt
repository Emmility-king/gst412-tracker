Welcome & Manual Guidelines
This practical laboratory manual is designed by the ICT Directorate of Wellspring University to equip students with industry-standard digital marketing, virtual assistance, and practical artificial intelligence skills.

•	Hands-On Execution: Students must perform all laboratory exercises directly during scheduled practical sessions.
•	Worksheet Documentation: Complete all exercises, prompt setups, design wireframes, and data tables directly inside this manual.
•	Supervisor Sign-Off: Each weekly lab session must be graded and signed off by the assigned Lab Supervisor.
•	Requirements: Access to a working computer/laptop, internet connection, and free-tier access to tools (ChatGPT/Gemini, Canva, Meta Business Suite, Mailchimp/Brevo, Trello, Google Workspace, Zapier/Make).

1. Practical Hands-On Example

"Act as the ICT Helpdesk Officer at Wellspring University. Draft a 150-word formal email guiding a new student on how to log into the student portal and reset their default password."
Task 2 (Data Extraction): Paste an unformatted email thread into the AI and request:

"Extract all dates, sender names, email addresses, and key action items from the text below and format them cleanly as a Markdown table."
